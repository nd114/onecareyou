# Notifications, privacy and control

## Choosing what you hear about

Settings lists what OneCare can tell you about, by **thing** rather than by
channel:

- **Medicine reminders** on this device.
- **Missed dose alerts** to the people in your care circle, by email.
- **Your adherence report**, in the app.
- **Account and security** email — this one cannot be switched off.

Two things worth knowing. A setting only appears if something actually sends it,
so there are no switches for mail that does not exist. And the one that cannot
be turned off says so on the row, with the reason: it is how you keep control of
your account, and there is no version of it you would want silenced.

Medicine reminders need two things: the setting here, and your browser or phone
allowing notifications. Both have to be on.

## Who has looked at your record

Your access history shows who opened your record, when, and which parts. It
records reading as well as changing.

This is a report the app makes, not something the database can prove on its own
— nothing in a database knows a page was rendered. What OneCare guarantees is
that a report cannot be forged or attributed to the wrong person; it cannot
guarantee that every read was reported.

## Your data, elsewhere

- **Export everything** from Settings, in a portable format.
- **Send a report** to a clinician as a PDF or CSV without giving them access to
  your account.
- **Delete your account.** Records a clinician created and is legally required
  to keep stay with them — that is the law rather than our choice — but your
  OneCare account and the data you put in it go.

## Simple version

OneCare briefly offered a "simple version" toggle. It has been withdrawn because
it did not do anything yet, and a switch that promises bigger text and delivers
nothing is worse than no switch. It will come back when there is something
behind it.
