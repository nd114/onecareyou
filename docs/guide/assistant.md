# The assistant

## What it is for

Three things:

1. **Questions about your own record.** "What am I taking?", "When is my next
   dose?", "What was my blood pressure last week?"
2. **Questions about a medicine.** Side effects, interactions, what to do about
   a missed dose.
3. **Doing things for you.** Log a reading, add a medicine, mark a dose taken,
   change reminder times.

## How it answers about medicines

It reads the drug label first and quotes it, and tells you which label. It does
not answer from memory.

If the label does not cover your question, it says so rather than filling the
gap — that is deliberate. A confident answer with nothing behind it is the
dangerous kind.

Interaction questions are checked against the NIH database and OneCare's own
reference. If that check cannot run, you will be told it could not run. **"No
interactions found" and "we could not check" are different answers**, and you
will never see the first when the second is true.

## Nothing happens without you

When you ask the assistant to change something, it **prepares** the change and
shows it to you. Nothing is saved until you tap Approve. The assistant is not
allowed to tell you a change is done before you have approved it.

If you approve something and it fails to save, you will be told that too.

## What it will not do

It does not diagnose, prescribe, or change a dose. Asked about a missed dose it
gives the label's own instruction where there is one, and otherwise tells you
not to double up and to ask a pharmacist — it will not work out a catch-up
schedule.

If you describe something urgent — chest pain, stroke signs, severe bleeding,
thoughts of harming yourself — it will tell you to call emergency services and
stop there.

## It is AI, and it can be wrong

Every conversation carries a line saying so. Answers are AI-generated, they are
general information rather than medical advice, and anything about your health
is worth checking with a licensed practitioner. That line is written by OneCare,
not by the assistant, so it is there whatever the assistant says.

The assistant only works if you turn on AI processing in Settings, and you can
turn it off again.
