# Getting started

## Create your account

Sign up with an email address and a password, or with a sign in partner. You will get a
confirmation email — open it before signing in. That step is not decoration:
some things in OneCare are released to whoever proves they own an address, so an
unconfirmed address gets nothing.

## Tell OneCare a little about you

Onboarding asks for your name, date of birth and country. The country matters
more than it looks — it sets your emergency numbers and whether you see
millimoles or milligrams, Celsius or Fahrenheit.

You can add conditions and allergies now or later. Allergies are worth doing
first: they are the thing a clinician most needs to see before they do anything.

## Put it on your home screen

OneCare works in a browser, but it is built for a phone. On the **Install** page
you will find the steps for your device. Once it is on your home screen it opens
straight into your day, works offline for reading, and can show you reminders.

## Your first four steps

A card on your dashboard walks you through four things, in order:

1. **Add your health basics** — conditions, allergies, who to call.
2. **Log a reading** — a blood pressure, a weight, whatever you have.
3. **Add a medicine** — what you take and when.
4. **Share with your doctor** — so somebody can see it.

Only the next step explains itself; the rest are titles. The card leaves on its
own when you have done all four, and you can dismiss it before then.
