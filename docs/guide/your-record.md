# Your health record

Everything in OneCare belongs to you. Nobody sees any of it until you say so,
and you can stop sharing at any time.

## Readings

Blood pressure, heart rate, glucose, weight, temperature, oxygen saturation and
lab values. Add one from the dashboard or from the Vitals page.

Every reading shows **where it came from** — you, a clinician, or an import from
a hospital system. That matters when two numbers disagree.

Readings are shown against a normal range where one exists. Some measurements
have no meaningful "normal" — weight is the obvious one — and OneCare says
nothing rather than inventing a band. If you see no range, that is why.

**Units.** Enter a reading in whatever unit your device shows. OneCare converts
and stores it consistently, so a temperature taken in Fahrenheit is not read as
Celsius later.

## Medicines

Name, dose, times, start and end dates, and who prescribed it.

- **Scan a pack** and OneCare will read the label and fill in what it can.
- **Interactions are checked** against the NIH database and OneCare's own
  reference. Combination products are checked ingredient by ingredient, so a
  warning about the ibuprofen inside a combined painkiller still appears.
- **Different names, same medicine.** Paracetamol and acetaminophen, salbutamol
  and albuterol, adrenaline and epinephrine — OneCare treats them as one drug.
- **Stopping a medicine** keeps it in your history rather than erasing it. What
  you used to take is part of your record.

## Documents

Letters, results, scans, insurance papers — anything. OneCare can summarise a
document in plain language and suggest a category.

**Archiving** puts a document away without deleting it. Your archive is still
yours, and a clinician you have given whole-vault access to does not see
archived documents.

Occasionally a clinic will withdraw a document they sent you in error — a letter
for somebody with the same name, for instance. When that happens the document
disappears from your vault and you are told: what date it was sent, when it was
withdrawn, and why. You will not see the contents again, and nor will anyone you
had shared your vault with.

## Recordings

You can record a consultation. The recording and its transcript are filed like
any other document — **except** that they are never included in whole-vault
sharing. Somebody who can see your whole vault still cannot hear your
appointments unless you share a recording deliberately.
