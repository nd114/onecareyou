# Sharing with your care team

## How sharing works

You choose a clinician or a hospital, and you choose **what** they see. The
categories are separate: readings, medicines, dose history, conditions,
allergies, your profile, documents. Granting one grants only that one.

Nobody at a hospital or a clinic can read your record without a share you
made. There is no emergency override.

## Sharing with one clinician

Send them an invitation from Care Circle. They see the categories you picked,
and nothing else, from the moment they accept. Only a clinician account can
accept an invitation.

What you share with a clinician personally stays between you and that
clinician. If they also work at a hospital, the hospital does not get it.

## Sharing with a hospital

A hospital is different from a person: staff change, and cover matters.

When you connect to a hospital with its code, the starting point is to share
**everything**, because clinicians treat you best with the full picture. The
screen where you connect says so plainly, and you can switch off any category
before you connect or at any time afterwards.

The people who can see your record are the clinicians the hospital has
assigned to you — or, at hospitals that have not switched that on yet, its
clinical staff. Reception and billing staff do not see your health record.
Either way it is still your categories that decide **what** they see.

Conversations with a hospital clinician belong to the hospital. If your
clinician leaves, the team now caring for you there can read the conversation
and carry it on, and you are told once who has taken over.

## Documents from your clinician or hospital

A clinician can send you a letter, a referral or a result. It arrives in your
Vault and you are told it has arrived. Each document says who sent it: "From
Dr Ada Obi · St Elsewhere General" from a clinician, or "From St Elsewhere
General (front desk)" when a hospital's reception or billing team sends you
paperwork such as a registration form. Reception and billing staff can send
paperwork only, never a result or a prescription. A file sent in a message can be saved to
your records too: right-click it on a computer, or press and hold on a phone,
and choose "Save to my records".

## Stopping

Revoke a share at any time, from Care Circle. It takes effect immediately:

- The clinician stops seeing your record on their next request, not at some
  point later.
- If a hospital had assigned a clinician to you, that assignment stops meaning
  anything the moment you revoke. The assignment stays as the hospital's record
  of who was responsible; it grants nothing.
- New messages stop too, in both directions, and the Messages screen tells you
  why. What was already said stays.
- What they already saw, they already saw. Revoking is not a time machine, and
  OneCare will not pretend otherwise.

## Sending a snapshot to someone without an account

To show part of your record to someone who is not on OneCare, send a snapshot
link from Care Circle. The person you send it to does not need an account. You
choose what goes in it. It is a copy of that moment,
not a live view, and it stops working after the time you set — 30 days at
most. You can add a passcode (three wrong tries lock the link for good), turn
the link off at any time, and see how many times it has been opened and when
it was last opened.

## Your sharing history

Care Circle keeps a record of every share you have granted, changed and ended,
with dates. If you ever need to say who could see what and when, it is there.
