# OneCare — Investor One-Pager

**The shared health record that patients and clinicians write together.**

---

## Problem
US healthcare loses **$26B/year to readmissions** because the record breaks the moment a patient leaves a clinical setting. Patients get read-only portals from EHRs they don't own. Clinicians can't see what happened between visits. Families managing an aging parent have no shared view. Every existing platform is either clinician-only (EHRs, SimplePractice, Elation) or patient-only (MyChart, Apple Health) — no one builds the shared surface.

## Wedge
**OneCare is the first canonical health record patients own and clinicians co-author with permission.** Patient signs up → invites their clinician via Care Circle → both write to the same record with granular consent (private, provider-scoped, family-scoped, fully shared). The record ports across providers because the patient owns it.

## Moat
1. **Patient-owned portable record** — no incumbent can pivot to this without cannibalizing their vendor lock-in
2. **Family-scoped care accounts** — market gap for adult children managing parents
3. **Interop-native (QHIN/TEFCA + FHIR)** — built for the world where records travel
4. **Explicit granular AI consent + 13-pattern PII de-identification** — regulator-favorable positioning
5. **Two-sided viral loop** — each clinician brings ~200 patients; each patient brings ~3 family caregivers

## Product (shipped)
- Patient app: vitals, medications, schedule, health vault, care circle, family switcher, AI assistant (Simple Mode), offline PWA
- Clinician app: RBAC + practice management, patient panels, Triage Inbox, encounters (SOAP), templates, audit log, compliance packs, referrals, internal notes
- Shared: secure messaging, per-document sharing with signed URLs, hybrid consent model, HIPAA-aligned (AES-256, TLS, RLS, audit logging)
